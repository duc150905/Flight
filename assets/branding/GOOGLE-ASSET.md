# Google sign-in icon

`google-sign-in.png` is the official 4× light icon-only square asset from:
https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip

Archive entry:
`Android + Web/PNG @4x/Light/Theme=Light, Show text=No, Shape=Square, Platform=Android+Web@4x.png`

Downloaded 2026-11-03 from Google's
[sign-in branding guidance](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines).
The complete PNG is retained unchanged. `GoogleIcon` displays its original center
G glyph at 20 × 20 inside the outlined white sign-in button; it does not recolor
or stretch the mark. No external image request is needed at runtime.
